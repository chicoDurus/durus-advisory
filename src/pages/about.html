About

Built for platforms that have to hold.

Durus Advisory works with leadership teams running complex digital platforms: several brands, several markets, regulation, and a lot of moving parts that all have to ship together.

Durus is Latin for hard, firm, unyielding. It's what we bring to product work that's gone soft: the discipline to make a call, the muscle to see it through, the honesty to know if it worked.

We've spent years inside multi-brand, multi-market platforms in regulated industries: owning roadmaps under commercial pressure, running delivery across engineering, compliance and marketing, and answering to the numbers at the end of each quarter.

That's the work we do now, for companies that need senior product judgment without adding a permanent executive. If you need a deck, hire a consultant. If you need the thing to actually get built, and to know whether it moved the needle, talk to us.

How we think.

Business results come first

Velocity, story points and burn-down charts are tools for the team. What we answer for is the effect on revenue, retention, cost and risk.

Process should be light

We keep the skeleton that helps: two-week sprints and a short daily standup. Any other meeting has to produce a decision or unblock work, or it goes.

Scrum isn't for everyone

Some teams do better with Kanban or a mix of both. We pick the lightest setup that fits how the work actually arrives.

Ship when it's ready

Release dates fixed months ahead ignore the state of the work. We release when it's built, tested and safe, in small and boring steps.

Trust the people doing the work

We don't micromanage. Teams decide how to get there, report clearly, and we step in only when something is actually off track.

Say it straight

If a plan won't work, we say so early. If we're not the right fit for a problem, we say that too.
