## When it makes sense

Product decisions have outgrown the people making them. Usually it looks like one of these:

- the roadmap is a list of requests from whoever asked last
- the founder or CTO is acting as product owner, and it's eating their week
- engineering ships steadily, but revenue, retention or conversion doesn't move
- several brands or markets on one platform have started drifting apart
- a product lead has left and the work is spread across people with full jobs already

A permanent head of product is a slow and expensive hire, and a costly one to get wrong before you know exactly what the role needs. Fractional leadership covers the gap properly, and often shows you what the permanent role should be.

## What we do

We own the roadmap and the prioritisation process. We work directly with engineering, design, compliance and the commercial side, set a short list of priorities tied to business outcomes, and make the trade-offs that keep the product focused.

Your team keeps running its own day-to-day work. We don't micromanage. We expect clear reporting, and we step in when something is actually off track.

## What you get

- a roadmap the team can deliver, ranked against real capacity
- one clear process for how new requests get decided
- measurement in place for the work that matters
- a weekly written update and a monthly review against agreed goals
- a product function that keeps running after we step back

## Format

Typically two to three days a week, on a rolling monthly basis, with a minimum of three months. The first month is mostly understanding, the second direction, the third delivery against the new priorities. From there it scales up, down, or hands over to a permanent hire.

## Questions

### Will you work inside our tools and team?

Yes. We use your ticketing, documentation and communication tools, join the meetings that matter, and work alongside your people as part of the team.

### How do we measure whether it's working?

We agree two or three business outcomes at the start, and report against them every month. Shipping features doesn't count on its own.

### Can you help us hire a permanent head of product?

Yes. By the end of an engagement we usually know exactly what that role needs, and we can help write the brief and assess candidates.
