Good products die in the gap between strategy and delivery.

The roadmap is agreed but nothing ships. Features go out with no clear reason. Teams stay busy without moving the numbers that matter. Strategy and delivery quietly drift apart.

Durus closes that gap. Here's how we plug in.

Fractional product leadership

Embedded product ownership for teams that need senior judgment without a permanent hire. We run the roadmap, unblock delivery, and keep engineering, compliance, and commercial pulling the same direction.

Product & platform audits

A hard look at what you've built. Where the funnel leaks, where brands have drifted out of parity, what's fragile, and what's worth fixing first, delivered as a prioritised plan your team can act on.

Strategy that reaches execution

Turning goals into a roadmap that actually ships. We shape what to build and why, make the trade-offs, and stay close enough to delivery that the strategy survives contact with reality.

Measurement you can trust

Making sure the numbers tell the truth. The right things tracked, funnels understood, and decisions grounded in data instead of vanity metrics, so you know whether the work worked.

How we work.

Direction

Turning goals into a roadmap teams can actually deliver. Sharpening what to build and why, prioritising against real constraints, making the trade-offs that keep a product focused instead of bloated.

Understanding

Getting to the bottom of what's actually going on. Listening to teams and stakeholders, mapping how the product really works today, and surfacing the gaps that no one's saying out loud.

Results

Making sure the work moves the numbers that matter. The right things are tracked, funnels are understood, and product decisions rest on data you can actually trust, not vanity metrics.

Uplift

Closing the gaps that quietly cost you. Benchmarking brands or products against each other, fixing what's fragile, and lifting quality across the board so the platform runs cleaner than it did.

Scale

Building the muscle to keep growing. Multi-brand, multi-market delivery without the whole thing coming apart at the seams. Senior product judgment on tap, without adding to headcount.

Who's behind it.

Durus means hard, firm, unyielding. It's what we bring to product work that's gone soft: the discipline to make a call, the muscle to see it through, the honesty to know if it worked.

We've spent years inside complex, multi-brand platforms in regulated markets. We've owned roadmaps under real pressure, shipped features that had to survive contact with compliance, marketing, engineering, and the CEO all at once. That's the work we do. That's the work we're good at.
