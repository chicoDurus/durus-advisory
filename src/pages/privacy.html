Legal

Privacy policy.

Last updated 6 October 2026. This page explains what personal data durusadvisory.com collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Who we are

This website is run by Durus Advisory, based in Cyprus. For anything about your personal data, email durus@durusadvisory.com.

What we collect

We keep data collection to a minimum. The site has no contact forms, no user accounts and no advertising trackers, and it doesn't set cookies.

  • If you email us, we receive your name, email address and whatever you include. We use it only to reply and, if we work together, to run the engagement.
  • Booking a call takes you to Calendly, which collects the details you enter under its own privacy policy. We receive those details to hold the meeting.
  • The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, it processes technical data such as IP addresses and browser type to deliver pages and keep the service secure.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with Google.
  • If we measure site traffic, we use privacy-friendly analytics that work without cookies and only report aggregated numbers.

Why we use it

To answer your messages, to prepare and hold calls, to run engagements, and to keep the website working and secure. Our legal basis is either taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, or our legitimate interest in running the business and the site.

How long we keep it

Correspondence is kept for as long as it's needed for the conversation or engagement, and afterwards only as long as we're legally required to keep business records.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers named above, who process data on our behalf or under their own terms. We don't sell personal data or share it for marketing. Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, under the safeguards required by GDPR.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive a copy of it. Email us and we'll respond within one month. You can also complain to the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection in Cyprus.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
