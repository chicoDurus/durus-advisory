How we work

The five things we focus on in every engagement, the formats we work in, and what a normal week with us looks like. We keep process light and spend the time on decisions.

Direction

Turning goals into a roadmap teams can actually deliver. Sharpening what to build and why, prioritising against real constraints, making the trade-offs that keep a product focused instead of bloated.

Understanding

Getting to the bottom of what's actually going on. Listening to teams and stakeholders, mapping how the product really works today, and surfacing the gaps that no one's saying out loud.

Results

Making sure the work moves the numbers that matter. The right things are tracked, funnels are understood, and product decisions rest on data you can actually trust, not vanity metrics.

Uplift

Closing the gaps that quietly cost you. Benchmarking brands or products against each other, fixing what's fragile, and lifting quality across the board so the platform runs cleaner than it did.

Scale

Building the muscle to keep growing. Multi-brand, multi-market delivery without the whole thing coming apart at the seams. Senior product judgment on tap, without adding to headcount.

What working with us looks like

  1. A first conversation

    You tell us what you're building and where it's under strain. We tell you straight whether we can help. If we can't, we say so.

  2. A written scope

    A short proposal: the problem, the outcome we're aiming at, the format, and how we'll both know it's working. No long statement of work.

  3. The first two weeks

    We get inside the product, the data and the backlog, talk to the people who matter, and come back with what we found and what we'd do first.

  4. Weekly reporting

    A short written update every week: what moved, what's at risk, what we need from you. Your team keeps running its own day-to-day. We step in where something is actually off track.

  5. Monthly review

    Results against the goals we agreed, in business terms. Then we adjust direction, scale the engagement up or down, or wrap it up with the team set to carry on without us.
