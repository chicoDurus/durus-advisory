Good products die in the gap between strategy and delivery.

The roadmap is agreed but nothing ships. Features go out with no clear reason. Teams stay busy without moving the numbers that matter. Strategy and delivery quietly drift apart.

Durus closes that gap. Four ways we plug in:

Fractional product leadership

Embedded product ownership for teams that need senior judgment without a permanent hire. We run the roadmap, unblock delivery, and keep engineering, compliance and commercial pulling the same direction.

For scale-ups and multi-brand platforms between product leads, or not ready for one. Learn more →

Product & platform audits

Where the funnel leaks, where brands have drifted out of parity, what's fragile, and what's worth fixing first, delivered as a prioritised plan your team can act on.

For new leadership, stalled growth, slowing delivery, or before a big bet. Learn more →

Strategy that reaches execution

Turning goals into a roadmap that actually ships. We shape what to build and why, make the trade-offs, and stay close enough to delivery that the strategy survives contact with reality.

For leadership teams with clear ambitions and a roadmap that keeps slipping. Learn more →

Measurement you can trust

Making sure the numbers tell the truth. The right things tracked, funnels understood, and decisions grounded in data instead of vanity metrics, so you know whether the work worked.

For teams that ship a lot and can't say what any of it changed. Learn more →
