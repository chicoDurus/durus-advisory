About Durus Advisory

We work with leadership teams running complex digital platforms. Usually that means several brands or markets, some regulation, and a lot of parts that have to ship together.

Durus is Latin for hard. The name is a reminder of what we're there for. Most product problems we see come down to a decision nobody made, or one that was made and then quietly not delivered.

We've owned roadmaps on multi-brand, multi-market platforms in regulated industries, run delivery with engineering, compliance and marketing in the same room, and had to explain the quarter's numbers afterwards.

Now we do that work for companies that need senior product judgment without hiring a permanent executive. We don't do strategy decks on their own. If you need something built and want to know whether it paid off, talk to us.

How we think

Business results come first

Velocity, story points and burn-down charts are tools for the team. What we answer for is the effect on revenue, retention, cost and risk.

Process should be light

We keep the skeleton that helps: two-week sprints and a short daily standup. Any other meeting has to produce a decision or unblock work, or it goes.

Scrum isn't for everyone

Some teams do better with Kanban or a mix of both. We pick the lightest setup that fits how the work actually arrives.

Ship when it's ready

Release dates fixed months ahead ignore the state of the work. We release when it's built, tested and safe, in small and boring steps.

Trust the people doing the work

We don't micromanage. Teams decide how to get there, report clearly, and we step in only when something is actually off track.

Say it straight

If a plan won't work, we say so early. If we're not the right fit for a problem, we say that too.
